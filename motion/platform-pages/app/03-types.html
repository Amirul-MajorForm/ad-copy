Campaign types

App campaigns across the whole journey

Some campaigns get your app discovered, some win the store search, and some turn downloads into regular users. We set the mix by your goal and budget.

  1. Stage 1

    Get discovered

    • Meta app ads

      Video and image ads in Facebook and Instagram feeds, Stories and Reels that open your app store page.

      CPICTR
    • TikTok app campaigns

      Short videos in the For You feed, made to feel native and drive downloads.

      CPICTR
    • Google App campaigns

      One campaign across Google Search, Google Play, YouTube and Discover, built from your text, images and videos.

      CPI
  2. Stage 2

    Win the store search

    • Apple Ads

      Ads at the top of App Store search results for the words people type, including your own app's name.

      Cost per tapTap-to-install rate
    • Store page tests

      Icon, screenshot and description tests, so more people who visit your store page install.

      Store conversion rate
  3. Stage 3

    Grow active users

    • In-app action campaigns

      Campaigns that aim for sign-ups, bookings or first purchases rather than downloads.

      CPACost per first purchase
    • Value campaigns

      Bidding towards people likely to spend more, once purchase data is coming through.

      ROAS
    • Re-engagement

      Ads that bring lapsed users back, with links that open the right screen in the app.

      Reactivation rateCPA

One plan, three stages. Budget moves to whichever stage is holding growth back.
