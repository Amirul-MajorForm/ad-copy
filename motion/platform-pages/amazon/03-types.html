Campaign types

Amazon campaigns across the whole funnel

Some campaigns put your products in front of new shoppers, some win the searches that lead to a sale, and some protect and grow the customers you have. We set the mix by your goal and budget.

  1. Stage 1

    Get discovered

    • Sponsored Display

      Ads on and off Amazon that reach shoppers browsing similar products or your category.

      CPMDetail page views
    • Amazon DSP

      Display and video ads bought with Amazon's shopper data, on Amazon and across other sites and apps.

      CPMReach
  2. Stage 2

    Win the search

    • Sponsored Products

      Product ads in search results and on product pages, the core of most Amazon accounts.

      ACOSCPC
    • Sponsored Brands

      Your logo, a headline and several products together at the top of search results.

      ACOSNew-to-brand
    • Sponsored Brands video

      Short video ads in search results that show the product in use.

      CTRACOS
  3. Stage 3

    Protect and grow

    • Brand defence

      Ads on your own product pages and brand searches, so competitors can't take shoppers who are already yours.

      ACOSImpression share
    • Remarketing

      Brings back shoppers who viewed your product but didn't buy.

      ROAS
    • Brand Store traffic

      Sends shoppers to your branded store on Amazon, where they see your full range.

      Store visitsSales

One plan, three stages. Budget moves to whichever stage is holding growth back.
