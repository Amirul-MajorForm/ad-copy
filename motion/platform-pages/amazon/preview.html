How Amazon advertising works

How Amazon ads work, and how we work with them.

On Amazon, people are already shopping, and your ad sits right next to the buy button. Doing well takes three things: showing up for the right product searches, product pages that turn clicks into sales, and knowing what each ad really earns. Here's how we handle each one.

  1. Show up for the right searches

    Most Amazon sales start with a search, and where your ad shows depends on your bid and how well your product sells for that search.

    How we handle it

    We research the words shoppers use, split campaigns by brand, category and competitor searches, and cut terms that spend without selling.

  2. The product page closes the sale

    An ad can only bring shoppers to your page. Weak images, unclear titles or few reviews mean they click and leave, and you still pay.

    How we handle it

    We review your titles, images, A+ content and stock levels, and pause ads on products that aren't ready to sell.

  3. Measured on sales, not clicks

    Amazon's reports only count sales made soon after an ad, and ads also lift organic sales that the reports don't show.

    How we handle it

    We report on ACOS alongside total ACOS (ad spend against all sales), so you see what ads add to your whole Amazon business.

One team, all three steps. Ads, listings and data, working together on your store.

01

How much do Amazon Ads cost in Singapore?

Most Amazon ads charge per click, with the price set in an auction each time a shopper searches. It depends on how competitive your category is and how well your product sells. We set a target for each product from its margin, agree a test budget with you, then scale what sells at a profit. Our fee is a monthly retainer, not a commission on spend.

02

What is ACOS, and what should ours be?

ACOS is your ad spend divided by the sales those ads brought in, so a lower number means each sale cost less in ads. A good target depends on your margin: if a product makes 30% profit before advertising, an ACOS above 30% loses money on each ad sale. New launches often run higher for a while to build sales history.

03

Sponsored Products, Brands or Display: which should we use?

Most accounts start with Sponsored Products, because they show your product right where shoppers search. Sponsored Brands put your logo and several products at the top of results, which helps shoppers remember you. Sponsored Display reaches people browsing similar products and brings back those who viewed yours.

04

Do Amazon ads help our organic ranking?

They can. Amazon doesn't publish how it ranks products, but sales for a search are widely seen as a strong factor, so ads that bring in sales for a search can help your product rank for it without ads over time.

05

Do we need Amazon Brand Registry?

Sponsored Products works without it. If you sell on Amazon as a seller, you need Brand Registry for Sponsored Brands, your own Brand Store and A+ content, so it's worth setting up if you own your brand.

06

Can you help with our product listings?

Yes. Ads work best on strong product pages, so we review your titles, images, bullet points and A+ content, and flag listings that need work before we put spend behind them.

07

How do you measure results on Amazon?

We track ACOS and return on ad spend for each campaign, and total ACOS, which compares ad spend with all your Amazon sales, to show what ads add overall. We also watch new-to-brand sales, which shows how many buyers are new to you.

08

How long before Amazon ads show results?

Ads can start bringing in sales within days of launch. Bids and keywords need a few weeks of sales data to settle, and organic ranking gains build more slowly, so we review results weekly and judge the bigger picture month by month.
