Campaign types

Google Ads campaigns across the whole funnel

Search captures people who are ready to act, while other campaign types build the demand that searches come from. We set the mix by your goal and budget.

  1. Stage 1

    Build demand

    • YouTube

      Video ads that introduce your brand to the right people before they start searching.

      CPVView rate
    • Demand Gen

      Image and video ads across YouTube, Discover and Gmail, built for new audiences.

      CPMCTR
  2. Stage 2

    Capture searches

    • Non-brand search

      Ads for the problems and products people search for, before they know your name.

      CPCConversion rate
    • Brand search

      Protects your name in results, so competitors don't win the people already looking for you.

      Impression shareCPC
    • Dynamic Search Ads

      Ads written from your website's pages to catch searches your keywords miss.

      CPA
  3. Stage 3

    Make the sale

    • Lead generation

      Search and call ads that bring in enquiries, judged on qualified leads rather than form fills.

      CPLCost per qualified lead
    • Shopping

      Product ads with image, price and shop name, straight from your product feed.

      ROAS
    • Performance Max

      One campaign across Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display and Maps, driven by your goals and feed.

      ROASCPA
    • Remarketing

      Brings back people who visited your site but didn't buy or enquire.

      CPA

One plan, three stages. Budget moves to whichever stage is holding growth back.
