How search advertising works

How search works, and how we work with it.

On Google, people tell you what they want before you show them an ad. Doing well there takes three things: being there for the searches that matter, giving Google's bidding the right signals, and knowing which clicks turned into customers. Here's how we handle each one.

  1. Show up for the right searches

    Broad match and Google's AI now decide which searches trigger your ads, so the wrong terms can quietly use up the budget.

    How we handle it

    We plan keywords around buying intent, organise campaigns by what people search for, and check search terms every week to cut waste.

  2. Google's bidding sets the price

    Smart Bidding sets a bid for every auction based on how likely each search is to convert, and it only learns from the conversions it can see.

    How we handle it

    We give bidding clear targets and clean conversion data, and test ad copy and landing pages so each click is worth more.

  3. Measured on leads and sales

    A form fill or a click on a phone number is not a customer. Optimising to the wrong action fills the pipeline with leads that never buy.

    How we handle it

    We track the actions that matter, feed lead quality and sales back to Google where possible, and report on cost per customer and revenue.

One team, all three steps. Strategy, copy, media and data, working together on your account.

01

How much does it cost to run Google Ads in Singapore?

It depends on your industry and how competitive your keywords are, because you pay per click and clicks in some categories cost far more than others. We estimate costs from keyword research before launch, agree a test budget with you, then scale what converts. Our fee is a monthly retainer, not a commission on spend.

02

How long before Google Ads show results?

Search can bring in enquiries within days of launch, because it reaches people already looking. Smart Bidding usually needs a few weeks of conversion data to settle, and clients typically see clear improvements in cost per lead or ROAS within 30 to 60 days.

03

Should I use Performance Max or Search campaigns?

Usually both, for different jobs. Search gives you control over keywords and messaging for high-intent queries, while Performance Max finds buyers across all of Google's channels using your product feed and goals. We set the split based on your products, data and targets.

04

Why bid on my own brand name?

Competitors can bid on your brand name, and ads appear above your website in the results. Brand campaigns are usually low-cost and protect the people already looking for you, though we check whether they are bringing in extra customers before recommending the spend.

05

Do you write the ads and advise on landing pages?

Yes. We write and test the ad copy, and review the pages your ads send people to, because a strong landing page often lowers the cost of every lead or sale more than bidding changes do.

06

Google Ads or Meta: which should I start with?

Start with search if people already look for what you sell, because you reach them at the moment of intent. Meta suits building demand for products people don't yet search for. Many clients run both, with each one feeding the other.
