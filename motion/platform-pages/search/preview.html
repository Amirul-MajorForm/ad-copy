How search advertising works

How search works, and how we work with it.

On Google, people tell you what they want before you show them an ad. Doing well there takes three things: being there for the searches that matter, giving Google's bidding the right signals, and knowing which clicks turned into customers. Here's how we handle each one.

  1. Show up for the right searches

    Broad match and Google's AI now decide which searches trigger your ads, so the wrong terms can quietly use up the budget.

    How we handle it

    We plan keywords around buying intent, organise campaigns by what people search for, and check search terms every week to cut waste.

  2. Google's bidding sets the price

    Smart Bidding sets a bid for every auction based on how likely each search is to convert, and it only learns from the conversions it can see.

    How we handle it

    We give bidding clear targets and clean conversion data, and test ad copy and landing pages so each click is worth more.

  3. Measured on leads and sales

    A form fill or a click on a phone number is not a customer. Optimising to the wrong action fills the pipeline with leads that never buy.

    How we handle it

    We track the actions that matter, feed lead quality and sales back to Google where possible, and report on cost per customer and revenue.

One team, all three steps. Strategy, copy, media and data, working together on your account.

Questions brands ask about Google Search ads

What brands usually ask us before we start running Google Ads together.

01

How much do Google Search ads cost in Singapore?

You pay each time someone clicks your ad, and the price is set in an auction every time a search happens. It depends on how many advertisers want that keyword, your bid, and your Quality Score, Google's rating of how relevant your ad and landing page are to the search. We estimate cost per click from keyword research before launch, agree a test budget with you, then scale what converts. Our fee is a monthly retainer, not a commission on spend.

02

How do you choose which keywords to bid on?

We start with the searches that show someone is ready to buy or enquire, such as your product, your service plus a location, or your brand name, and set match types so your ads show for the right variations. We add negative keywords to block searches you don't want to pay for, like jobs or free versions, and check the search terms report regularly to add new winners and cut the waste.

03

Why bid on my own brand name if I already rank first?

Competitors can bid on your brand name, and their ads appear above your website in the results. Brand campaigns usually cost little per click and keep people who are already looking for you on your page. We still check whether they bring in extra customers before recommending the spend.

04

Can you reach people searching near our outlets or studios?

Yes. We target by location and can show your address in the ad. For brands with several outlets, we run location searches in their own campaign with ads written for each outlet, as we did for Yoga Movement's studios, so someone searching nearby sees the place closest to them.

05

Should I use Search, Shopping or Performance Max?

It depends on what you sell. Search gives you control over keywords and messaging for high-intent searches, and suits services and lead generation. Shopping and Performance Max use your product feed to show products with prices and images, which suits e-commerce. Many brands run Search alongside one of them, and we set the split from your products, data and targets.

06

Should we do search ads or SEO?

Search ads put you at the top of the results from the day they go live, and you choose the message and the page people land on. SEO earns unpaid rankings but takes months to build. Many brands run both, and the keyword data from your ads shows which searches turn into sales, which helps decide what to write about for SEO.

07

How do you measure leads and sales from Google Ads?

We set up conversion tracking for the actions that matter to you, such as purchases, enquiry forms and calls, using Google Ads and GA4. Google's bidding then aims for those actions rather than clicks, and we report on cost per lead or sale, not clicks or impressions.

08

How long before Google Ads show results?

Search can bring in enquiries within days of launch, because it reaches people who are already looking. Smart Bidding usually needs a few weeks of conversion data to settle, and clients typically see clear improvements in cost per lead or ROAS within 30 to 60 days.
