What's changed on Meta

Meta's AI now decides who sees your ads, using broad audiences and systems like Andromeda. What it can't do is make better ads, so results depend on the range and quality of creative you give it.

What used to work

  • Detailed interest and lookalike targeting
  • Many small, tightly split ad sets
  • One or two hero ads a month

What works now

  • Broad audiences and Advantage+ campaigns
  • Fewer, simpler campaigns that can learn
  • A steady flow of distinct creative angles

Meta Ads FAQs

How much does it cost to run Meta ads in Singapore?

It depends on your goal and market. Meta's delivery works best when each ad set gets about 50 conversions a week, so we size the test budget around that, agree it with you before launch, then scale what works. Our fee is a monthly retainer, not a commission on spend.

How long before Meta ads show results?

Most accounts leave Meta's learning phase within one to two weeks. Clients typically see clear improvements in CPA or ROAS within 30 to 60 days, once creative testing has found winners.

What is Meta's Andromeda update, and what should I change?

Andromeda is the system Meta uses to choose which ads to show each person. It favours accounts with many genuinely different ads, so the biggest lever is now creative variety: different angles, hooks and formats, not small edits of one ad.

Should I use Advantage+ or manual campaigns?

Usually both. Advantage+ sales campaigns find buyers efficiently at scale, while manual campaigns give control for launches, specific audiences and tests. We split budget based on your catalogue, data and goals.

Do you make the ad creative in-house?

Yes. Our production team makes static, video and UGC-style ads for Facebook and Instagram placements, and we read results by creative, not just by campaign, so every round of ads learns from the last.

Meta or TikTok: which should I start with?

Start where your customers already spend time and where you can make enough creative. Meta suits most brands first because of its reach and retargeting; TikTok suits brands that can feed it native, creator-led video. Many clients run both.
