What's changed on Meta

Meta has changed. Here's how we work with it.

Meta's AI now does the targeting. It reads every ad, matches it to the people most likely to act, and learns from what happens next. Each of those steps needs a different skill, so here's how we handle each one.

  1. Creative that feeds the algorithm

    Meta can only find the right people through the ads it's given, and Andromeda now rewards many genuinely different ads over one polished hero.

    How we handle it

    Our in-house production team makes and tests new angles, hooks and formats continuously.

  2. Meta's AI finds the audience

    Broad audiences and Advantage+ have replaced most manual targeting. The AI needs room and budget to learn.

    How we handle it

    We build fewer, broader campaigns with clear testing rules, so the AI learns fast instead of stalling.

  3. Measured on business outcomes

    Meta learns from the results it can see, and its own reports don't always match what reaches your bottom line.

    How we handle it

    We agree the outcomes that matter to you, like revenue, ROAS or cost per customer, and report against them, not clicks or reach.

One team, all three steps. Strategy, creative, media and data, working together on your account.
