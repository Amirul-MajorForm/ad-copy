Campaign types

Meta campaigns across the whole funnel

Most accounts need a campaign at each stage. We set the mix by your goal and budget, then judge each campaign on its own numbers.

  1. Stage 1

    Get seen

    • Reach

      Shows your ads to as many of the right people as possible, with frequency control.

      CPMAd recall
    • Video views

      Builds an engaged audience from people who watch your videos, ready to retarget.

      Cost per ThruPlayView rate
  2. Stage 2

    Build interest

    • Traffic

      Sends the right people to your site, app or landing page.

      CPCLanding page views
    • Lead campaigns

      Instant forms or your own site, for services, education and property.

      CPL
    • Messaging

      Starts conversations in WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram.

      Cost per conversation
  3. Stage 3

    Make the sale

    • Advantage+ sales

      Formerly Advantage+ Shopping. Finds buyers at scale from your catalogue.

      ROAS
    • Manual sales

      Tighter control of audiences, placements and offers.

      CPA
    • Catalogue retargeting

      Brings back people who viewed your products but didn't buy.

      ROAS
    • Collaborative Sales Ads (CPAS)

      Runs your ads with retail partners like Shopee or Lazada, sending shoppers straight to your products on their store.

      ROASRetailer sales

One plan, three stages. Budget moves to whichever stage is holding growth back.
