01

How much does it cost to run TikTok ads in Singapore?

It depends on your goal and market. TikTok sets minimum daily budgets, and its algorithm needs enough conversions to learn, so we size the test budget around that, agree it with you before launch, then scale what works. Our fee is a monthly retainer, not a commission on spend.

02

How long before TikTok ads show results?

Most campaigns leave TikTok's learning phase within the first week or two. Clients typically see clear improvements in CPA or ROAS within 30 to 60 days, once creative testing has found winners.

03

What are Spark Ads?

Spark Ads run real TikTok posts, from your account or a creator's, as ads. The likes, comments and follows stay on the original post, and because they look like normal content, people are more likely to watch them.

04

Do you run TikTok Shop and LIVE campaigns?

Yes. We run GMV Max campaigns for TikTok Shop and promote LIVE sessions to drive shop sales. Our TikTok livestreaming work with Blackmores delivered a 4X ecommerce ROI.

05

Do you make the TikTok creative in-house?

Yes. Our production team makes native, sound-on videos for TikTok, from hooks and product demos to UGC-style ads, and we read results by creative, not just by campaign, so every round of videos learns from the last.

06

TikTok or Meta: which should I start with?

Start where your customers already spend time and where you can keep making video. TikTok suits brands that can feed it native, creator-led video; Meta suits most brands first because of its reach and retargeting. Many clients run both.
