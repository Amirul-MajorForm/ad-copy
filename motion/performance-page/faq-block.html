01

How is MajorForm different from a traditional media agency?

We're paid a retainer, not a commission on your ad spend. That gives our team the time to focus on strategy, testing and decisions backed by data across Meta, Google, TikTok and other platforms, and to shape the work around your goals, from building your brand to driving sales.

02

Who is MajorForm for?

Fast-growing startups, DTC brands and regional businesses that want to grow profitably through digital channels. That could mean launching in a new market, getting more from your ad spend or building growth that lasts. We tailor the work across three areas: brand, performance and commerce.

03

What platforms does MajorForm manage?

Meta (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok, LinkedIn and Google (Search, Performance Max, YouTube and App Install), plus commerce platforms like TikTok Shop, Amazon and Google Shopping, and programmatic partners such as StackAdapt, Teads and Criteo. We don't favour any one channel: we find what works for your audience and scale it.

04

What kind of results can I expect with MajorForm?

It depends on your goals, but most clients see better CAC, ROAS and creative performance within the first 30 to 60 days. We test quickly, learn from every round and move budget to what converts, with clear reports so you can see what's working.

We plan each market on its own terms. Creative, media strategy and platform mix are localised to how mature the market is, what customers cost to acquire and how people use each channel, across Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, the Philippines and beyond. From launch to scale, we handle the complexity so you can grow with confidence.
