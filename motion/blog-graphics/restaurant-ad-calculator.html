Is this the same as profit margin?

No. Contribution margin only subtracts the costs that rise with each extra diner. Net profit margin also subtracts fixed costs such as rent, salaries and utilities, so it is always lower.

About these assumptions

Published restaurant advertising benchmarks generally measure leads or actions, such as form fills, rather than genuinely incremental diners. For example:

  • WordStream / LocaliQ, Google Ads Benchmarks 2026: US$30.57 cost per lead for Restaurants & Food in US Google and Microsoft search advertising.
  • WordStream / LocaliQ, Facebook Ads Benchmarks 2025: US$3.16 cost per lead for Restaurants & Food in US Facebook lead campaigns.

These are US-dollar lead-generation figures, not Singapore cost-per-diner benchmarks, so they should not be entered into this calculator directly. Not every lead becomes a diner, and not every diner is additional.

Each additional diner contributes S$18 before advertising. If each one costs S$30 to win, a S$300 budget brings in about 10 diners and S$180 in contribution, S$120 short of the spend. To cover it, you would need at least 17 diners, or a cost per diner of S$18 or less.
